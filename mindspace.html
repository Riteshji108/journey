How it works ↓

🔒 Nothing you write ever leaves this browser.

The problem

Reflection rarely happens on its own.

Most people mean to check in with themselves — and most days, it just doesn't happen. Feelings pile up unexamined, and the habit that would help the most is the one that keeps slipping.

The solution

A small, honest ritual.

MindSpace gives you one calm place to write, name a mood, and see it laid out over time — with guided prompts when you don't know where to start, and a breathing exercise when you just need a minute.

The impact

Awareness, without exposure.

Every entry stays on your device, in your browser's storage. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or shared — so the only cost of building the habit is a few honest minutes.

MindSpace is a self-reflection tool and is not a substitute for professional mental-health care.

Your privacy

Your journal entries stay on this device and are not sent to a server.

MindSpace has no backend, no account, and no analytics. Everything you write is stored using your browser's local storage, on this device only. Clearing your browser data, or using a different device or browser, will not carry entries over. You can permanently delete everything from the Dashboard at any time.

Delete all journal data?

This permanently removes every entry stored on this device, including demo entries. This can't be undone.
